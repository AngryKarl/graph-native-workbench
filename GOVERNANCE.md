# Governance

Graph Workbench is currently maintainer-led and evidence-driven.
Maintainers merge changes, publish releases and protect the Product Charter.

Public contract changes require an issue or RFC describing the user problem,
alternatives, compatibility impact and why an adapter or Pack is insufficient.
Decisions favor the smallest mechanism that supports at least two credible
domains. Significant decisions are recorded as ADRs.

Consistent contributors may become reviewers or maintainers based on judgment,
technical stewardship, respectful collaboration and sustained responsibility—not
employer, geography or volume of commits. Governance will become more explicit
as the maintainer group grows.
