# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want a rigorous, welcoming community where domain experts, operators,
designers and developers can disagree constructively. Be respectful, explain
trade-offs, assume good intent while responding to impact, and make space for
people with less context.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing and sustained disruption are unacceptable. Technical
criticism must address work and evidence, never personal worth or identity.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public. Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue warnings, temporarily
restrict participation or permanently ban participants when necessary.

Report harmful behavior privately to a maintainer using the contact method on
their GitHub profile, or use GitHub's built-in abuse reporting for urgent cases.
Reports will be handled confidentially to the extent practical. Maintainers
involved in an incident must recuse themselves from its resolution.
